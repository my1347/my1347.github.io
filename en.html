Jiantao Liu刘键涛

Jiantao Liu is a Distinguished Professor in the College of Mechanical Engineering and Automation at Huaqiao University, Director of the Key Laboratory of Port Logistics Equipment and Intelligent Control, Ministry of Transport, Chief Scientist at Fujian Port Technology Group, and Co-Founder, CTO and Deputy General Manager (joint appointment) of HIRI FUTURE. A Leading Talent of the Ministry of Transport and a Fujian Young Top-notch Talent, he works across physical AI, artificial intelligence, computer vision, embodied AI and equipment intelligence, focusing on physical-world perception, precision measurement and autonomous decision-making.

He established the first AI team in China's port sector, growing it to more than 120 researchers and engineers. His team has advanced high-precision visual localization, state estimation in complex operating conditions and other core technologies, building a fully domestic technology stack from sensors and chips to algorithms. The resulting technologies serve more than 100 enterprises, with 4,000+ intelligent equipment installations across 100+ ports in six countries, including China, Singapore, Brazil, Thailand, Malaysia and Sri Lanka. Technology commercialization has generated an output value exceeding RMB 1 billion.

He has led two national MIIT “Challenge and Lead” innovation programs, both recognized as national winners. He is a two-time First Prize (Class A) winner of the China Artificial Intelligence Competition, as first contributor, and received the Second Prize of the Fujian Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award, also as first contributor. He has published more than 30 SCI-indexed papers, with research appearing in leading AI venues including IEEE TPAMI and ECCV, and has 900+ Google Scholar citations with an h-index of 19.

Liu earned his Ph.D. from Fuzhou University and his M.S. from the University of Pittsburgh. He also supervises graduate students at Xiamen University, Fuzhou University, Jimei University and Xiamen University of Technology.

Commercialization

Co-Founder · CTO · Deputy General Manager

HIRI FUTURE focuses on physical AI, developing intelligent port equipment, AI-enabled safety systems, domain-specific foundation models and embodied AI. It is a national-level Specialized and Innovative “Little Giant” Enterprise, a National High-Tech Enterprise, and a Fujian Provincial AI “Leading Goose” Enterprise. Its independently developed stack spans core algorithms, domestic chips and sensors, and has received full domestic-stack Xinchuang certification from CESI under the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.

Its products and solutions now serve more than 100 ports across six countries, advancing conventional terminals toward fully autonomous operations.
